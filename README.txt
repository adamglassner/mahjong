HOW TO PLAY
Clear the board two matching tiles at a time. A tile is free when nothing
lies on top of it and its left or right side is open. Tiles match when they
are the same; any Flower matches any Flower and any Season any Season.

Hint shows a pair you can take, Shuffle mixes the tiles that are left (the
board always stays clearable) and Undo takes back a pair. Finish without
hints or shuffles for three stars.

KEYS
H hint, U or Ctrl+Z undo, S shuffle, N new deal, M music, Esc back,
F11 full screen.

PUZZLES
Easy: Lotus Pad, Stepping Stones, Little Lantern, Koi
Medium: Moon Gate, Arched Bridge, Paper Fan, Tea House
Hard: Stone Turtle, Jade Dragon, Great Pagoda, Garden Fortress

SAVES AND UPDATES
Your game, best times and settings are saved in
%APPDATA%\Ironite\Mahjong\save.json.
This copy checks GitHub for updates and can install them for you
(Settings > Updates). If you play through Ironite Launcher, the launcher
keeps Mahjong up to date instead.
