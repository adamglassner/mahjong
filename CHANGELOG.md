# Changelog

All notable changes to Mahjong - Jade Moon Garden are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added
- Mahjong solitaire in a moonlit Chinese garden: clear the board two matching tiles at a time.
- Twelve puzzles: four Easy (Lotus Pad, Stepping Stones, Little Lantern, Koi), four Medium (Moon Gate, Arched Bridge, Paper Fan, Tea House) and four Hard (Stone Turtle, Jade Dragon, Great Pagoda, Garden Fortress).
- Every deal can be cleared. Hint, Shuffle (keeps the board clearable) and unlimited Undo.
- Stars for each puzzle, best times, and your game is saved when you close the window.
- Three scenes (Moon Garden, Lantern Festival, Plum Blossom Dawn) and three tile sets (Ivory & Jade, Celadon, Rosewood), all drawn sharp at any window size.
- Four original tunes played live on zither, bamboo flute and soft strings, plus tile clicks, chimes and a gong for a cleared garden.
- Updates itself from GitHub (the standalone copy), and installs and updates from Ironite Launcher.
